Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Certify has no sign-up and no login. You can start studying right away, and your preferences are saved locally on your device.

Does the app work offline?

The app interface works offline, but lessons load from the web the first time you open them, so an internet connection is needed to fetch new lesson content.

How do I change text size or reduce motion?

Open Settings inside the app. There you can adjust text size, turn on Reduce Motion, manage notifications, and view your downloads.

How is my privacy handled?

Certify does not collect personal information. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

I found a bug or have a feature request

We'd love to hear it. Email naqvim@myumanitoba.ca with a short description and, for bugs, the steps that led to the problem.
